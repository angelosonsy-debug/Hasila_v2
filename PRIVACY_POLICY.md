# Privacy Policy — حصيلتي (Hasila)

**Effective date:** 2026-08-01
**Developer:** Angelos Onsy

---

## 1. What the app is

Hasila (حصيلتي) is a personal English vocabulary learning app that uses
spaced-repetition to help you review words and situational phrases at the
right time.

---

## 2. Data collected

**Hasila itself collects no personal data and has no accounts or servers.**
Your learning data stays on your device (section 3). The only exception is
the ads shown by Google AdMob (section 4) - rewarded video ads you choose to
watch, and a small banner shown on the Settings and Add-word screens -
which involve data that Google collects.

| Category | Collected by the app? | Collected by Google AdMob (rewarded + banner ads)? |
|---|---|---|
| Name, email, or account information | No | No |
| Location | No | Approximate location (derived from IP address) |
| Photos, camera, microphone | No | No |
| Contacts or calendar | No | No |
| Device identifiers (Android advertising ID) | No | Yes |
| Device / ad-interaction data (e.g. ad views, diagnostics) | No | Yes |
| Crash reports or analytics by the app | No | No |
| Your vocabulary, progress, settings | Stays on your device | No |

---

## 3. Where your data lives

Everything you enter in the app — vocabulary, review history, settings,
streak — is stored **only on your device**, in the app's private local
storage (Android localStorage via the Capacitor WebView). The app never
uploads it. If you use "Export backup", the file is created on your device
and handed to the share sheet you choose; where it goes after that is up
to you.

---

## 4. Internet access and advertising

The app requests `INTERNET` permission so that ads can be loaded and shown
through Google AdMob: **optional rewarded video ads** (for example, to
unlock an extra daily review session or earn a streak freeze - shown only
when you tap the button to watch one) and a **small banner ad** shown on
the Settings and Add-word screens (never during a review session). Google
AdMob may collect and use the
advertising ID and related device data to serve and measure ads, under
Google's own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy . You can reset
or opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
Fonts are bundled inside the app; no font requests are made.

---

## 5. Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification
using the Android OS notification scheduler. No notification data is sent
to any server. You can disable reminders at any time inside the app's
Settings, or revoke the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission in your Android
system settings.

---

## 6. Third-party SDKs

| SDK | Purpose | Data sent externally? |
|---|---|---|
| Capacitor (Ionic) | WebView bridge | No |
| Google Text-to-Speech (system) | Word pronunciation | No |
| Google AdMob (rewarded + banner ads) | Optional rewarded video ads; banner ad on Settings/Add screens | Yes — advertising ID, device and ad-interaction data, collected by Google |

---

## 7. Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
The app is not directed to children. It shows ads (rewarded + banner, via Google AdMob) and has no social features.

---

## 8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be available in the app's
Google Play listing and in this file (PRIVACY_POLICY.md) in the source
repository. The effective date at the top will be updated.
